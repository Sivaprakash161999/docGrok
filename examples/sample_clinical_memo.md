# Clinical Evaluation Memo: Regional Tele-Health Robotic Program
**Document ID:** TEST-MEMO-404
**Department:** Surgical Innovations
**Date:** 2024-08-10

## Executive Summary
This internal memorandum outlines the pilot deployment of tele-robotic surgical consultation kiosks across three rural satellite hospitals.

## Budget and Expenditure Details
- Initial Hardware & Camera Rig Outlay: $95,000.00
- Software Licensing & HIPAA Encryption Tunnel: $18,500.00
- Total Program Pilot Cost: $113,500.00

## Patient Outcomes
- Consultations completed: 142 patients
- Emergency transfers averted: 29 cases
- Patient satisfaction rating: 96.4%
